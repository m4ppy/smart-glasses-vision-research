# Paper

## Title

**Performance Comparison of YOLO-Based Hand Shape Recognition for Smart Glass Environments**

## Korean Title

**스마트 글래스 환경을 위한 YOLO 기반 손 모양 인식 성능 비교**

## Status

Manuscript prepared for submission.

## Summary

This paper compares YOLOv8 and YOLOv12 for 26-class alphabet hand-shape detection and supplements the comparison with a separate YOLOv8n experiment.

The additional experiment analyzes:

- training convergence over 100 epochs
- validation and independent test performance
- class-wise recognition errors using a normalized confusion matrix

The study also discusses the applicability and limitations of YOLO-based hand-shape recognition for smart-glass environments.
